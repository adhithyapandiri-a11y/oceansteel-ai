How OceanSteel AI Works (In 60 Seconds)

SIH 2026 Problem Statement: SIH262006

1. The Problem: $25,000/Day Waiting Fines

India imports 70+ Million Tonnes of coking coal every year to make steel. When ships arrive at crowded ports like Paradip, they often wait 7 to 10 days in line before they can unload. Every waiting day costs $25,000 USD (₹21 Lakhs) in fines (demurrage).

2. The Solution: Connecting Sea & Train Data

OceanSteel AI catches the ship 10 to 14 days early while it is still in open water. It checks every port on the East Coast (waiting lines, water depth) AND checks Indian Railways train wagons.

3. The Result: ₹2+ Crore Saved Every Voyage

It directs the ship to an empty port (like Dhamra) where trains are already waiting. The coal reaches the steel factory 8 days faster, saving over ₹2.26 Crore in fines and rail costs on a single ship!
