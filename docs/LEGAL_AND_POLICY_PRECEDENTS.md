# OceanSteel AI: Statutory, Legal & Policy Precedents

This document details the legal, regulatory, and policy foundations underpinning **OceanSteel AI** (SIH262006).

---

## 1. Calcutta High Court Ruling (2026)
### *Steel Authority of India Ltd. (SAIL) vs. Vizag Seaport Private Ltd.*
* **Legal Citation:** [https://indiankanoon.org/doc/171502422/](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/171502422/)
* **Significance in OceanSteel AI:**
  The High Court affirmed the charterer's legitimate prerogative under standard charter party liberties clauses (e.g. BIMCO GENCON Clause 18 / C(ORE)7) to revoke an initial port nomination and redirect a dry bulk vessel to an alternative safe deep-water port when the nominated berth suffers from unreasonable, foreseeable detention or congestion.
* **Application in System:**
  OceanSteel AI references this precedent directly in its automated **BIMCO Charter Party Rerouting Directive** when diverting incoming Capesize vessels from congested ports (e.g. Paradip) to deep-draft automated facilities (e.g. Dhamra Port).

## 2. Supreme Court of India Landmark Demurrage Ruling (2020)
### *Union of India & Anr. vs. Indian Inland Waterways & Ors.*
* **Legal Citation:** Civil Appeal No. 17957/2012, Judgement dated 12-Feb-2020  
  [Supreme Court Judgment PDF](https://main.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2012/17957/17957_2012_33_1501_20697_Judg_12-Feb-2020.pdf)
* **Significance in OceanSteel AI:**
  The Supreme Court emphasized the doctrine of mitigation of damages in maritime transit and port operations. Parties possessing predictive information regarding port bottlenecks bear a commercial and legal duty to mitigate demurrage liabilities where feasible through intermodal diversion and advance coordination.
* **Application in System:**
  Provides legal justification for Indian Public Sector Undertakings (SAIL, RINL) to proactively issue diversion orders 10–14 days in advance to protect public exchequer funds.

## 3. Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways & DGS Orders
* **Directorate General of Shipping (DGS) Circulars:**
  Establishes guidelines on waiver of detention and demurrage charges during force majeure and operational gridlocks, mandating seamless data exchange between Port Trusts, customs stevedores, and Indian Railways.
* **Application in System:**
  Ensures that OceanSteel AI's automated **Notice of Readiness (NOR)** and **CRIS FOIS Electronic Rake Indents** comply with statutory port conservancy guidelines.

## 4. National Logistics Policy (NLP)
* **Goal:** Reduce India's logistics cost from **13–14% of GDP** to **single digits (8–9%)** to match developed economy benchmarks.
* **Contribution of OceanSteel AI:**
  * Raw material transportation represents up to 40% of the cost of crude steel production in India.
  * By eliminating avoidable port demurrage fines ($20,000–$35,000/day) and taking advantage of shorter railway routes (e.g. Dhamra $\rightarrow$ Rourkela is 44 km shorter than Paradip $\rightarrow$ Rourkela, saving ₹70/MT on Class 140 rail tariffs), the platform directly cuts logistics expenditure per metric tonne of steel.

---

## 5. National Steel Policy 2017 & 300 MTPA Vision
* **National Vision:** Scale crude steel production capacity to **300 Million Tonnes Per Annum (MTPA)** by 2030–31.
* **Contribution of OceanSteel AI:**
  * Scaling to 300 MTPA requires over **180 Million Tonnes of coking coal** annually, over 80% of which must be imported via East Coast ports.
  * OceanSteel AI prevents blast furnace feedstock stockouts, ensuring that steel mills maintain their critical 12-day inventory runway.
  * Prevents the drain of foreign exchange (Forex) reserves, since demurrage on foreign bulk carriers must be paid in US Dollars.
