How OceanSteel AI Works: 1-Minute Executive Guide

Smart India Hackathon 2026 • Problem Statement ID: SIH262006

The Critical Problem Solved

Indian steel plants import over 70 Million Tonnes of coking coal annually from Australia. Due to sudden port berth breakdowns and railway wagon shortages, foreign Capesize bulk carriers wait up to 7 to 10 days at outer anchorage on India’s East Coast. This costs steel companies $25,000 USD/day in demurrage penalties, wasting millions in foreign currency (Forex) and threatening blast furnace shutdowns.

The Groundbreaking Solution

OceanSteel AI is India's first unified Maritime-to-Rail decision engine. While existing tools (like MarineTraffic) only track ship GPS, OceanSteel AI actively combines live AIS vessel positions with Indian Railways (FOIS/CRIS) rake availability and port queue forecasts to calculate the exact mathematical Total Landed Cost (TLC).

⚖️ Mathematical Decision Gate

The algorithm triggers a DIVERT signal 10–14 days in advance in open ocean whenever:
Demurrage Saved > Extra Sea Fuel + Rail Freight Delta
